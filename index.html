    Before you write

    A few questions, answered

    How long does an invitation take?

    A ready design, made personal, usually takes one to two weeks. A Couture invitation, created from the first sketch, takes three to six weeks. Tell us your wedding date and we’ll plan backwards from it.

    How do our guests receive it?

    As a video made to play beautifully on WhatsApp, and, if you choose a wedding website, a link that opens on any phone with every function, venue, map and RSVP in one place.

    Can we make changes?

    Yes. Every invitation includes rounds of refinement, and we show you the first cut on your own phone before anyone else sees it.

    Which languages do you work in?

    Hindi and English, together or apart, with names and blessings in Devanagari. Ask us about other languages.

    How are these made?

    We use professional design tools, including AI, and every invitation is art-directed, personalised and finished by our team, frame by frame.
