01 — The Darbar

आमंत्रण

An invitation is the first scene of a family’s story.

Aamantran is Hindi for invitation. An atelier is the couturier’s workshop. Together they promise an invitation made by hand, with the patience of couture.

Our look is the Palace Parlour — an old-world Rajasthani parlour, patterned and collected. Yours will always be your own: every piece we make for a family is bespoke.

05 — The Atelier

Not a template shop

Every piece is art-directed. We compete on taste and craft, and we give each family the time that takes. This is how an invitation comes to life.

  1. I

    Your letter

    Write to us, and we reply within a day with our lookbook.

  2. II

    A conversation

    A call about your families, your functions and the feeling you want guests to have.

  3. III

    Moodboard & storyboard

    Motifs, palette and lettering, and every reveal timed to your music.

  4. IV

    The first cut

    Your invitation in motion, to watch on your own phone first.

  5. V

    Refinements

    We adjust until every frame feels like yours.

  6. VI

    Sealed & delivered

    Delivered under the chhatri seal, ready for every family group.

  7. VII

    A year later

    An anniversary greeting, from us to you.
